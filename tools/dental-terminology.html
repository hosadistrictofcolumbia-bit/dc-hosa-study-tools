Simulate the Test draws questions in exactly these proportions. Tooth numbering and surface nomenclature are not a separate topic, but they run through everything — learn them first.

Spelling counts in the tiebreaker

In the event of a tie, successive sets of five tiebreaker questions are judged until a winner emerges. Correct spelling is required for an item to count. That is unusual and it is decisive — practice writing dental terms out by hand, not just recognizing them in a list.
