Half of this test is the CDC Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report — twenty-five questions on real reports published in the window the guideline points to, not on textbook epidemiology. You cannot cram that from a study guide alone. Read MMWR weekly from now until your conference, learn the anatomy of a report, and practise pulling the case definition, the study design, the key rate and the recommendation out of each one. CDC — Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report

cdc.gov/mmwr · free · 50 percent of the test

Published every Thursday. Full reports, Notes from the Field, Surveillance Summaries and Recommendations and Reports. Subscribe to the email and read one report a week between now and your conference — that habit is worth more than any other preparation for this event.
