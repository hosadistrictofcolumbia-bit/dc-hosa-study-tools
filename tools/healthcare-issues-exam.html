What the guideline does specify is four sources and three date windows, and questions come from articles those sources published inside your window. The habit beats the cram

The four official sources

Named in the 2026-2027 guideline · questions come from these and nowhere else

This is unusually specific for a HOSA event: not "read health news" but four named sites. Read all four weekly through your window. Between them they cover consumer health reporting, academic public health, research press releases, and clinical explainers — four different registers, and questions come from each.

Background sources that make the news readable

Not on the guideline list — but they are what the news assumes you already know

Most health headlines are a study result, a regulatory decision, or an outbreak report. These free sources explain the machinery behind all three, which is what the first six topics of this guide are built on.
