Read this before anything else. Nutrition and Physical Activity are 25 percent each — half the test between them. But the half that decides medals is Round Two: you pick one personal healthy-living goal, work it, and document it in a printed portfolio, then present for five minutes — shortened this year from longer. A portfolio that shows eight weeks of real self-monitoring beats a beautiful one assembled the week before. Pick your goal today.

The official 26-27 test plan

Simulate the Test draws questions in exactly these proportions. Mental Health is only 10 percent of the written test, but the behavior-change material inside it — SMART goals, Stages of Change, self-monitoring — is what your portfolio is built from. Do not skip it because the percentage is small.

How the two rounds combine

Round One qualifies you; Round Two is where the placement is decided. Bring a printed hard copy of the portfolio — there is no screen to present from — and optional notecards. Judges score the portfolio itself as well as the presentation, so it has to stand on its own after you leave the room.

The HOSA Healthy Living course by NASM

National Academy of Sports Medicine · the only official reference

This event has no textbook. NASM built a course specifically for this competition that follows the test plan unit by unit — PDFs, articles, videos and blogs. If you work through the units that match the five topics, you have covered the written test. Nothing else on this page replaces it.

Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans

U.S. Department of Health and Human Services · free

The federal standard behind the 25 percent Physical Activity block: the weekly aerobic and muscle-strengthening targets, what counts as moderate versus vigorous, and the guidance by age group. Free, short, and quotable.

Dietary Guidelines for Americans and MyPlate

USDA and HHS · free

The federal standard behind the 25 percent Nutrition block. Note that a new edition of the Dietary Guidelines was released in January 2026; the NASM course was built on the previous edition, so learn the enduring principles and be aware the consumer graphic has changed.
