Read this before anything else. Two topics — the Legal Environment and the Health Care Environment — are 35 percent each. Seventy percent of your score sits in those two blocks. Bioethical Issues adds another 25 percent, which leaves just 5 percent for the definitional basics. Study accordingly: this is a law test with an ethics section, not the reverse.

The official 26-27 test plan

Simulate the Test draws questions in exactly these proportions. The 5 percent basics topic is small but it is the vocabulary the other three depend on — do not skip it just because it is only two or three questions.

How ties are broken

In the event of a tie, the essay is judged. It is administered with the original test inside the same 60 minutes, so plan for it rather than discovering it at minute 58.
