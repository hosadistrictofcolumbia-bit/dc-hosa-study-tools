Start here. The topic worth the most is the one people do not expect: medical nutrition therapy at 20 percent. That means the diets people follow when they have diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease or celiac disease — food as part of the treatment. Add carbohydrates, fats and proteins at 15 and vitamins and minerals at 15, and three topics carry half the test.

The official 26-27 test plan

Simulate the Test draws questions in exactly these proportions. Cultural and religious influences is 5 percent — one or two questions — but it is easy to learn and easy to get right.

How the test works

Round One is 35 questions in 60 minutes with a tiebreaker given in the same booklet, so budget time for it before you start. This is a Middle School Division event — you are competing against other middle schoolers, not against high school members.
