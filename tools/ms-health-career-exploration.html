Start here. The first five topics are the five health career clusters — Health Informatics, Therapeutics, Diagnostics, Support Services and Biotechnology. The last five are skills every health worker needs no matter which cluster they end up in. Ten topics, 10 percent each, so each one is three or four questions. Nothing here can be skipped, and nothing needs more than an evening.

The official 26-27 test plan

Simulate the Test pulls three or four questions from every topic, every time. A flat plan means you always know what a weak spot costs you.

How the test works

Round One is 35 questions in 60 minutes with a tiebreaker given in the same booklet, so budget time for it before you start. This is a Middle School Division event — you are competing against other middle schoolers, not against high school members.
