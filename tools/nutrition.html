Read this before anything else. Medical nutrition therapy is 20 percent of the test — the largest single block — and it is organized by condition: diabetes, renal disease, cardiovascular disease, GI disease, cancer, and clients with special needs. Learn the therapeutic diet for each of those six and you have banked a fifth of the exam.

The official 26-27 test plan

Simulate the Test draws questions in exactly these proportions. Carbohydrates/fats/proteins, vitamins and minerals, and lifespan nutrition are 15 percent each — together with medical nutrition therapy they are two thirds of the test.

How ties are broken

In the event of a tie, the essay is judged. It is administered with the original test, so it comes out of the same 60 minutes.
