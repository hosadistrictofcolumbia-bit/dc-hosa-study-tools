Rounding rules that decide points

The guideline sets the arithmetic conventions explicitly, and they are worth more than they look. Round only the final answer, after every step. Five or more rounds up, four or less stays. Unless told otherwise, round to the nearest whole number — but pediatric doses always round down to avoid overdose. Decimals below one take a leading zero (0.5, never .5), and whole numbers never take a trailing zero (5, never 5.0). In the event of a tie, the essay is judged.
