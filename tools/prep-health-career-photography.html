Health Career Photography

You photograph three different health professionals in three different fields actually doing their jobs, write a one-page narrative about each career, collect a signed facility permission form and a signed patient/subject permission form for every photo, bind it all into a portfolio, upload a copy as one PDF, and then present the three printed photos to judges in five minutes.

You submit
One combined PDF of the photography portfolio, uploaded by the competitor to the HOSA Digital Upload System by the DC HOSA upload deadline. The PDF is a copy of the physical portfolio: title page, then for each of the three photos in order a photo page, a one-page narrative, and the two permission forms. Separately, you bring the physical portfolio — in an official HOSA portfolio or notebook from The HOSA Store, with each 8" x 10" photo in a clear sheet protector — to the conference for the required display time and for Round Two.
Rounds
Round One is the digital upload of the portfolio, pre-judged virtually in early June from your PDF alone. Round Two is a presentation to judges of no more than five (5) minutes, roughly a minute and a half per photo. Only the top competitors from Round One in each division advance; how many advance depends on Round One criteria and on the space and time available for Round Two, and finalists are announced on-site at ILC. Separate from both rounds, every competitor at ILC must attend the required Project Display Time, standing with the portfolio and sharing the experience with delegates. Scores from Round One are added to Round Two for the final result.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS." That is HOSA's national ILC upload deadline; competitors who do not upload are not eligible for Round Two and will not be given a competition appointment time at ILC.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. Pre-judging and advancement to Round Two has been clarified. Competitors now have a maximum of five (5) minutes for their presentation with judges. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. Every photo needs two signed permission forms — a facility permission form AND a patient/subject permission form — and they live in the portfolio behind that photo's narrative. That is six signed forms for three photos, and you cannot produce any of them after the fact. Print the forms from the end of the official guideline PDF and take them with you to the shoot; get the facility signature from whoever can actually authorize it and the subject signature from the person in the frame before you leave. If you shoot all three photos at the same facility, you still put a copy of that facility form behind each of the three narratives. Plan every shoot around who signs, and remember all photos must be HIPAA-compliant — no identifiable patient, no visible chart, screen, wristband or name board.

    What Judges Score

    Round One is pre-judged virtually in early June from your uploaded PDF alone — no one meets you and no one can ask what a photo is supposed to show, so the photo and the narrative have to carry it. Round Two is the five-minute presentation to judges, and finalists are announced on-site at ILC. "Scores from Round One will be added to Round Two to determine the final results." A tie is broken by the rating sheet section with the highest point value, then the next highest, in descending order.     HIPAA and photographing in a health facility

    The guideline requires HIPAA-compliant photos

    Before you point a camera in a clinical space, know what cannot be in the frame: a patient's face, a name board, a chart, a monitor showing a record, a wristband. These are the federal sources a facility's own privacy officer works from, and reading them makes the permission conversation go faster.
