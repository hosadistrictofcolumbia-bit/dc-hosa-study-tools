Health Career Preparation

You write a short paper called a Statement of Interest, explaining why you want a career in healthcare, you upload it as one PDF, and then you talk with judges for five minutes about the same thing.

You submit
One thing gets uploaded: your Statement of Interest. It must be ONE PDF file. A PDF is a file that looks exactly the same on every device, so the judge sees what you made. It goes into the HOSA Digital Upload System. Nothing else is submitted, and nothing at all is carried into the interview room.
Rounds
Round One is your Statement of Interest. It is pre-judged, which means judges read and score it before the conference, on their own. Round Two is an interview with judges at the conference, lasting a maximum of five (5) minutes. Every competitor who uploads a Statement of Interest by the upload deadline advances to Round Two. A competitor who does not upload is NOT eligible for the interview and will NOT be given a competition appointment time at ILC.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress. This event also has a special rule about greeting: in Job Seeking Skills, Interviewing Skills, and Health Career Preparation only, handshakes between judges and competitors are allowed and encouraged per the rubric. In this event, handshakes are allowed both at the beginning and at the end.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS to receipt of the required materials after the deadline." That is the national ILC deadline for the International Leadership Conference.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. The interview has increased to five minutes. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. Your Statement of Interest is scored on how it looks, not just on what it says. The guideline prints an exact format and it is not a suggestion: Arial, 12 point font. Double-spaced. 1" margins. 8.5 x 11" paper, single-sided. The title page is one page only and everything on it is centered. The body is a maximum of two (2) pages, and those two pages do not include the title page. Every page after the title page needs a running header — that is a line of text that repeats at the top of every page — with your last name and the event name on the top left and a page number on the top right. The title page is not counted in that page numbering. On top of all that, the upload must be ONE PDF file. The guideline is blunt: "If the upload is not submitted as a single PDF document, the judge will NOT view it and NO points will be awarded." Set your document up this way on the very first day you start writing. Fixing the font, the spacing and the header the night before the deadline is exactly how good writing loses points.

    Setting up the format

    The part that quietly costs the most points

    Arial 12 point, double spacing, 1" margins and a running header are all built-in features of Google Docs and Microsoft Word. The one that needs help is turning the header off on the title page so page numbering starts on the next page. Sit down with your advisor or an English teacher for fifteen minutes and set it up once, at the start. Then write inside it.
