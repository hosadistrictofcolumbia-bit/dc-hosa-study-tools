Job Seeking Skills

You choose a real health-related job, internship, scholarship, or educational opportunity you are qualified for or being trained for, write a personal statement and a one-page resume for it, and then interview for it with judges.

You submit
ONE combined PDF file containing your Personal Statement and your Resume, uploaded to the HOSA Digital Upload System. Nothing else is submitted — and nothing is carried into the interview, because NO materials may be taken into the interview room, including hard copies of your own resume and personal statement.
Rounds
Round One is the Personal Statement and Resume, uploaded as ONE combined PDF and pre-judged virtually. Round Two is a mock job interview with judges, conducted for a maximum of five minutes. All competitors who upload materials by the upload deadline advance to Round Two; competitors who do not upload are NOT eligible for the interview and will NOT be given a competition appointment time at ILC.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress. This event also has an unusual rule about physical greeting: in Job Seeking Skills, Interviewing Skills, and Health Career Preparation only, handshakes between judges and competitors are allowed and encouraged per the rubric, both at the beginning and at the end of the event.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS to receipt of the required materials after the deadline." That is HOSA's national ILC date for material submitted through the Digital Upload System.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. This is the event where a strong competitor loses points on formatting. The Personal Statement has an exact specification and it is not negotiable: 12 pt. Arial, double-spaced, in English, 1" margins on 8 ½" x 11" paper, one-sided pages, a maximum of two pages, a running header with your last name and the event name on the top left, and a page number on the top right of each page — not counting the title page. The title page is page 1 and must carry seven specific fields. On top of that, the Personal Statement and the Resume must be uploaded as ONE combined PDF: "If the upload is not submitted as a single PDF document, the judge will NOT view it and NO points will be awarded." Set the document up correctly on the first day you start writing. Retrofitting Arial and a running header into a finished draft the night before DC's upload closes in the first week of February is exactly how this goes wrong.
