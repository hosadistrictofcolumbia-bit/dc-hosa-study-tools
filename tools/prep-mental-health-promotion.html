Mental Health Promotion

Your team picks a youth mental health topic, works with a Mental Health Consultant to identify the risk and/or protective factors behind it, actually builds and publishes a social media campaign for your peers, and then explains the whole process to judges in five minutes.

You submit
ONE COMBINED PDF FILE uploaded by one team member to the HOSA Digital Upload System, containing the summary presentation (title slide, completed Mental Health Consultant Form as an image, reference page(s) as an image, and examples of the campaign) with the public URLs to your live social media campaign typed or pasted into the PDF.
Rounds
Round One is the Digital Social Media Campaign, pre-judged virtually from your uploaded PDF before ILC — judges get seven minutes to review it. Round Two is a five-minute verbal presentation to a panel of judges at ILC. All teams who upload by the upload deadline receive an appointment time and advance to Round Two.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress. All team members must be properly dressed for the team to receive the bonus points.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states that May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline and that there will be NO EXCEPTIONS to receipt of materials after it. That is HOSA's national ILC upload deadline.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. The presentation requirements have been clarified. Presentation time has been updated. School and Campus Health website has been added as an event resource. Information regarding disabling comments on social media posts has been added to the GRRs. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. Two things end this event before it is judged. First, every link in your PDF must be set to PUBLIC permissions — the guideline says links without public permission result in the entire project being given a zero score, and judges must be able to view the content without logging in to any account. Second, the whole submission must arrive as ONE COMBINED PDF FILE; if it is not a single PDF, the guideline says the judge will NOT view it and NO points will be awarded. Third and quieter: the completed Mental Health Consultant Form is part of that PDF, and it depends on another adult's calendar. Start that conversation first, not last.
