2026–2027 Leadership Solo Event: 1 competitor Middle School, Secondary & Postsecondary/Collegiate

Prepared Speaking

You write and deliver a five-minute speech on one national topic that HOSA chooses for the whole year. For 2026-2027 that topic is “Redefine the Standard.” Nothing is uploaded and nothing is turned in ahead of time — the entire event is you, the judges, and five minutes.

You submit
Nothing. This event has no digital upload — the guideline lists digital upload as “NO.” What you present is a five (5) minute speech, given out loud to judges. You bring a photo ID, and notecards if you want them (paper or electronic, and optional). HOSA Conference Staff provide the equipment and supplies listed in Appendix I.
Rounds
This extract lists one round: Round One is a 5 minute Speech. It does not describe a Round Two, and it does not explain how competitors advance or how many are called back. Do not assume a second round exists or does not exist — check the official Prepared Speaking guideline PDF and ask your advisor.
Dress
This extract does not include a dress code section, so there is no attire rule and no dress bonus quoted here. Do not guess and do not copy the rule from another event. HOSA’s General Rules and Regulations and the official Prepared Speaking guideline PDF are the authority on what to wear. Read them, and ask your advisor before conference day.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline lists digital upload as “NO.” There is no national ILC upload deadline for this event, and nothing is ever sent to the HOSA Digital Upload System.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

Read this before anything else. Two things make this event unusual, and both are good news. First, the topic is already public: the guideline prints “2026-2027 Event Topic: Redefine the Standard.” You are not waiting for an announcement and you are not choosing a subject, so you can start writing today. Second, there is nothing to upload — no paper, no PDF, no deadline in May. Everything you are scored on happens live, in five minutes, in one room. Now the honest part: this guideline extract is short. It does not tell you the dress code, what happens if you run long, whether you may use posters or slides, or what the judges’ rating sheet contains. Those answers exist — they are in the official Prepared Speaking guideline PDF and in HOSA’s General Rules and Regulations. Download that PDF early and read it with your advisor. Anything this guide does not state, the PDF decides.
