Public Health

Your team builds a presentation about this year's public health topic, delivers it live to a real community audience, then films a three-minute video trailer that convinces judges they need to see the whole thing.

You submit
One team member uploads a link to the team's Round One video trailer to the HOSA Digital Upload System. The guideline requires a link, not a video file — and the link must already be set for public viewing.
Rounds
Round One is a three-minute video trailer, uploaded as a link by the deadline and pre-judged virtually in early June. Round Two is the full in-person presentation to judges at ILC, a maximum of 10 minutes, with three minutes to set up and three minutes to tear down. Between them sits a Required Project Display Time that every team must attend, whether or not they advance.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress. All team members must be properly dressed to receive bonus points — one teammate out of dress code costs the whole team the bonus.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "ONE team member uploads the portfolio to the HOSA Digital Upload System by May 15 for ILC competition." May 15 at midnight EST is the national final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. Pre-judging and advancement to Round Two has been clarified. Prep time has changed to three minutes prep and three minutes tear down. Presentations can now be a maximum of 10 minutes. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. This event has an assigned annual topic, and for 2026-2027 it is Fast-Check Your Feed: Taking Down Health Myths Before They Spread — health misinformation, what the World Health Organization calls an "infodemic." You cannot pick your own subject. Second: the presentation you show judges in Round Two must be the same presentation you actually gave to a live community audience, and you open Round Two by stating the date and audience out loud as proof. That means the community delivery has to happen months before ILC, not after. Book it now.
