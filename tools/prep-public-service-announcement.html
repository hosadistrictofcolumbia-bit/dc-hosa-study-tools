Public Service Announcement

Your team produces an original video public service announcement of 30 seconds or less on the 2026-2027 topic, airs it to a real audience, documents that it aired, and then spends five minutes telling judges how you made it.

You submit
ONE COMBINED PDF FILE, uploaded by ONE member of the team to the HOSA Digital Upload System, containing the reference page(s), the Copyright Form, the Air Date Form, and the Brain Health 101 and Brain Health 201 certificates for every team member. The link to the 30-second PSA video is uploaded separately as a hyperlink, and it must be set to public.
Rounds
Round One is the digital upload, pre-judged virtually: judges watch the PSA through the hyperlink you submit and review the combined PDF. Round Two is a presentation to judges at ILC — you show the PSA on your own battery-powered device, then have five (5) minutes to describe your creative process.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress, and all team members must be properly dressed for the team to receive them.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS." That is HOSA's national ILC date, and the guideline adds that competitors who do not upload are not eligible for Round Two and will not be given a competition appointment time at ILC.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. Presentation time has been changed to five minutes. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. More teams lose this event on paperwork than on filmmaking. Four things have to be true by the time DC HOSA's upload closes at the end of the first week of February, and none of them can be assembled at the last minute: every team member has completed HFCUniverse Brain Health 101 and Brain Health 201 and downloaded the PDF certificates; the PSA has actually been shown to a real audience and you have an Air Date Form signed by a school or community leader; you have written permission for any music, logo, or trademarked product in the video; and all of it — references, Copyright Form, Air Date Form, certificates — is fused into ONE combined PDF. The guideline is blunt about what happens otherwise: "If the upload is not submitted as a single PDF document, the judge will NOT view it and NO points will be awarded." The video itself goes in as a separate hyperlink, and it must be public — judges will not request access, and cannot score a link that does not open.
