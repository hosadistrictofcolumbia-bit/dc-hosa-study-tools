Research Poster

Your team poses a health-related research question about your own community, conducts original research to answer it, and presents the findings on a 48" x 36" landscape poster.

You submit
One PDF of the finished poster, uploaded by ONE member of the team to the HOSA Digital Upload System. For ILC you must also bring that poster professionally printed at 48" x 36" in landscape orientation.
Rounds
Round One is the digital upload of the poster, pre-judged virtually in early June. Round Two is a five-minute prepared oral presentation to a panel of judges at ILC, delivered standing next to your printed poster. Separately from Round Two, every team at ILC — advancing or not — must attend the required Project Display Time.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points are awarded for proper dress, and all team members must be properly dressed for the team to receive them.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS." That is HOSA's national ILC date for material submitted through the Digital Upload System.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. This event has moved to the Teamwork Category and is no longer a solo event. Teams will consist of two-four competitors. Teams now have five minutes to present to judges. Pre-judging and advancement to Round Two has been clarified. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. This event asks you to conduct research, not summarize somebody else's. The guideline is explicit: "The goal is for competitors to conduct their own research and not simply summarize someone else's." That means you collect your own data — a survey, interviews, a scientific study, an observational ethnography — from your own community, and it must all happen inside the current HOSA membership year, July 1, 2026 to May 15, 2027. It is also your team's responsibility to obtain informed consent from every participant. Formal IRB approval is not required, but ethical practice and proper documentation are expected. None of this can be retrofitted in April. Pick the question and start collecting data in the fall.

    Research ethics and consent

    Named by the guideline

    Formal IRB approval is not required for HOSA projects, but you are expected to follow ethical research practices, obtain informed consent from participants, and document content appropriately. The guideline points competitors to the HOSA FAQ for guidance.
