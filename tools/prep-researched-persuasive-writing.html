Researched Persuasive Writing & Speaking

You research the annual health topic, take a side for or against it, defend that side in a four-page research paper you upload, and then argue the same case out loud in a five-minute speech to judges.

You submit
The research paper, as ONE PDF file, uploaded to the HOSA Digital Upload System. Nothing else is uploaded, and nothing goes into the speech room with you except optional notecards — the guideline specifically bars the research paper itself from the speech.
Rounds
Round One is the research paper, uploaded to the HOSA Digital Upload System as one PDF by the deadline and pre-judged virtually. Round Two is a speech to judges, no more than five minutes, at an assigned appointment time. All competitors who upload a research paper by the upload deadline receive a Round Two appointment; competitors who do not upload are NOT eligible for Round Two and will NOT be given a competition appointment time at ILC.
Dress
Proper business attire or official HOSA uniform. Bonus points will be awarded for proper dress.

The DC HOSA calendar — read this before the guideline’s dates

Stage 1 · SLC — binding first

DC HOSA collects uploads and pre-judged material by the end of the first week of February. What you submit then must be final and complete — every required element, in the right format. It is judged exactly as submitted. The State Leadership Conference itself is in March, but the upload closes over a month before it. This is the deadline DC competitors miss.

Stage 2 · ILC — only if you qualify

Qualify at SLC and you may revise and improve the work. DC HOSA’s ILC upload deadline is generally around 1 May, deliberately ahead of the national date, so there is time to confirm every file landed in the right place and fix it if it did not.

For reference · the national deadline

The guideline states: "May 15 at midnight EST is the final deadline, and there will be NO EXCEPTIONS." That is HOSA's national ILC upload deadline.

Work to the DC dates, not this one. DC’s deadlines shift a little year to year — confirm the exact dates with your chapter advisor or the state advisor before you plan around them.

New for 2026-2027. Competitors now have a maximum of five (5) minutes for their speech with judges. Editorial changes have been made.
Read this before anything else. The topic is already set, so you can start today. The guideline prints it: "2026-2027 Event Topic: Should Pharmaceutical Companies Advertise?" You are not waiting on an announcement and you are not choosing a subject — you are choosing a side, for or against, and then defending it. The paper is tiny for how much research it needs: page 1 is the title page, pages 2-3 are the entire body, and page 4 onward is references. Two pages of argument means every sentence has to carry weight, and it means you cannot fix a vague thesis by writing more. Decide your stance early, build the source list, then write to fit. And note the format is prescriptive down to the header — 12 pt. Arial, double-spaced, 1" margins, running header top left, page number top right — so build the document shell before you draft a word.
