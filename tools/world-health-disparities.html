Barr — Health Disparities in the United States

Johns Hopkins University Press · latest edition · the one textbook

The only book on the reference list, and the one that supplies the conceptual framework: social determinants, the gradient between socioeconomic status and health, and how race, class, and place interact. Everything else on this list is a website.
